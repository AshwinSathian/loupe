## The third review, of the Astro rebuild

### 1. Summary

Reviewed: the whole site (/, /method, /cases, the three case pages, /testing, /tools, /specimen, /faq and the 404) as served by `wrangler dev` from `dist/` with the production `_headers`, plus the Astro source in `site/src`. No `.loupe.md` exists, so the review ran on an inferred profile (below) and did not write one.

Verdict: the design system is traced and the contrast work holds up (no contrast failures in 132 measured combinations), but several of the rebuild's new moving parts are broken: the home page's loupe specimen shrinks to a 2 × 2 px dot within three seconds of loading, at every width; the seal checker's skeleton is an empty 34 px box; the page skeleton hides the masthead; and the Copy buttons say nothing to a screen reader on success.

Most important: fix the loupe stage's resize feedback loop (finding 6) before this branch ships. It is the site's one signature element and the home page's proof, and once the rebuild is live it is a task that can't be completed in a release build, which is a Blocker. After that, the three loading states (4, 5, 21) and the two announcement and focus fixes (1, 3).

**Assumptions (no profile):** platform web; `release_stage: development`, because this is the unmerged `feat/astro-site` rebuild (findings 6 and 2 become harder once it ships); `register: utilitarian`, inferred from the core job (decide whether to install a developer tool, check a colour pair); voice taken from the existing strings: en-GB spelling, sentence case, curly apostrophes, contractions, "we" rare. `direction` comes from the repo's own design source, not a profile: the token comments in `src/styles/global.css` lines 23–28 and /specimen "Where the look comes from" (a lightbox and viewing-booth grey, a darkroom under a safelight, a redline kept for measured values). The single typeface, Bricolage Grotesque, for text, numbers and code is the owner's mandate. It was measured anyway, and anything it causes is labelled "owner's choice".

### 5. Distinctiveness verdict

**Authored**, with the caveat that its one signature element doesn't survive this build (finding 6).

Channels: colour is authored (viewing-booth grey, darkroom, safelight, and a redline used only on measured values; each traced in `global.css:23-28`). The signature element and iconography are authored (the loupe ring, the ring wordmark, the hero's dimension line, the crop marks on the one thing under inspection). Voice is authored (plain, specific, numbers instead of adjectives). Imagery is authored (a real fixture screenshot, not stock). Shape is authored (square frames, crop marks). Type is a trend default: Bricolage Grotesque is in the catalog's second-layer default tier, and with optical size pinned at 36 (finding 12) it carries little beyond weight 800 and −0.02 em tracking. Layout is a platform default (top navigation, single reading column). Motion is a trend default (a cross-document fade and a shimmer).

Tells counted: Bricolage as a default-tier face (type); a shimmer skeleton on content that resolves in under a second (the hero readout on a normal load; slop catalog "skeletons for content that loads in under a second"). That is 2 tells in 2 channels, under the cluster threshold of 6 across 3. `defaults` from measure.js is empty on every route.

Logo-off test: passes. The redline readout beside a self-measuring headline, the darkroom palette and the dimension line identify the site without the wordmark. Competitor-swap test: passes. Impeccable's page (before/after sliders, testimonial wall, star count) can't take loupe's measured hero or the pre-registered /testing page without the claims becoming false.

Direction (from the repo's design source; no profile): the site sits on it, apart from type, where the pinned optical size works against "a face drawn for the size it's set at". No theming sketch beyond finding 12's type move is needed.

### 8. Enhancements

Evidence is thin beyond the tools page, so there are four.

**Experience and flows**
- **Checker permalinks** (Flexibility). loupe's reports cite colour pairs ("#a9b0ab on white, 14 px"). Encoding `fg` and `bg` in the /tools URL (WebAIM's permalink, landscape) would let a report, an issue or the bookmarklet panel link straight to a loaded pair. It unlocks "Report a wrong finding" arriving with the pair attached.
- **Nearest passing colour** (Agency). loupe's own Fix column says "use the page's own --muted, which measures 5.56:1". The checker could do the same: under a failing verdict, show the closest OKLCH lightness of the foreground that clears 4.5:1 and 3:1, as a value the visitor can paste. It turns a verdict into a fix, which is the skill's method.

**Content and voice**
- **Seal rows that link to both files** (Responsibility). Each row of the seal table could link the key at the sealed commit and at `main`, so a doubter can open the two and diff them. The page's promise is "every claim links to the file behind it".
- **The headline readout admits the face's limit** (Responsibility). The hero already reports size and contrast measured live.
